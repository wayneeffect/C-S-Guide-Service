### About This Project

This is a clean, modern, dark-themed single-page website built as an updated version of the original [ccameron.biz](https://ccameron.biz/). It showcases **C & S Guide Service**, a family-owned bear hunting outfitter with deep roots in Michigan’s Upper Peninsula.

The redesign preserves the authentic voice and legacy of the Cameron family while delivering a contemporary look that feels at home on mobile devices and modern browsers.

### The Business

**C & S Guide Service** (Clarence & Son / Cameron family) is Newberry’s original bear hunting guide service. Started in the early 1960s by Clarence Cameron, who began hunting bears in the 1950s, the operation is now a three-generation family tradition involving Clarence, Kevin, and the rest of the crew.

**What they offer:**
- Trophy bear hunts north of Newberry, Michigan
- Hound hunts (featuring top Spring Creek Rock Breed Walkers and other proven lines)
- Bait hunts
- Guided experiences
- Lodging available
- Decades of local knowledge in the same prime territory

They emphasize hard work in the field (scouting starts mid-July), top-tier hounds and equipment, and treating hunters like buddies rather than customers. The service is proud of its record-book success and lifetime memories.

### Features of the Redesign

- Deep dark theme with high-contrast accents for a rugged, woods-at-night feel
- Clean, readable typography and mobile-first layout
- Streamlined navigation and content organization
- Prominent contact information and calls-to-action
- Family legacy and hound-hunting emphasis highlighted
- Fast-loading, simple static site (easy to host anywhere)

### Tech Stack

- HTML5 + CSS3 (custom dark styling)
- Responsive design
- Minimal JavaScript (if any – keeping it lightweight)

### Original Inspiration

The content and photos draw directly from the long-running Cameron family operation. This project modernizes their online presence while honoring the tradition of “hunting is in our blood.”
