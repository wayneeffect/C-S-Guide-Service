Daily hunts available

Daily hound hunts, daily bait hunts, and photo hunts are available when conditions allow. Contact us for the latest availability and pricing.

Custom options

We can bait your site before arrival and offer combo hunts with hound hunts in the morning and bait hunts in the afternoon.

Additional details

  • Field dressing, skinning, freezer, and camping space are available but not included in listed prices.
  • Rustic cabin accommodations may be available on request.
  • We use the finest breed and trained hounds in the state, including Spring Creek Rock Breed Walkers.
  • Bait hunts take place on active, guaranteed baits when available.

Notice: Some ground blinds are available. We do not provide tree blinds for liability reasons, but you may bring and set up your own after signing a liability waiver at camp.

Booking note: A 50% deposit is required for all hunts, and reference checks are available upon request.

Extra recreation: Within 25 miles there is excellent steelhead fishing at the mouth of the Big Two Hearted River, plus walleye and pike lakes. October hunters may also enjoy archery deer hunting after a successful bear hunt.

Special offers: Discounts for groups of 4 or more are available. Kids under 14 without a bear license hunt free.
