About us

Newberry’s first guide service

Clarence began bear hunting in the 1950s and started C & S Guide Service in the early 1960s. Today the family has more than 85 combined years of experience guiding hunters across Michigan’s woods.

We work extremely hard to make every trip memorable. Our hunting never stops — we scout trophy country starting July 15 and train our hounds year-round.

Our services

Hound Hunts
